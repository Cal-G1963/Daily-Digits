How it works

  1. Goal: combine all six numbers into one that matches the TARGET at the top.
  2. Combine: tap a number tile, tap a second one, then tap +, −, ×, or ÷. The two tiles flip together into one new tile.
  3. Subtraction & division: subtraction gives the positive difference either way; division only works when one number divides evenly into the other.
  4. Keep going until one tile is left, then see how close you landed.
  5. Hints: you get 3 a day — each one glows two tiles you could combine next.
  6. Streak: come back daily to keep it going. A new puzzle drops every day.

Stuck? Reset the puzzle any time before you finish — it won't cost you anything.
